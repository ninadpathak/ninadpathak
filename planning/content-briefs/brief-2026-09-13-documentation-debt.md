## Evidence — Experience B

The artifact is an audit run against a real corpus, and this site is one. Real, inspectable debt
found in August: 20 of 88 pages with no inbound link, nine with fewer than two outbound, 22
cross-cluster links of which six failed the subject test, and one page whose entire outbound link
profile was convenience links.

That last one is the most useful finding in the piece: `technical-content-as-a-moat` looked
connected and was not. A page can pass a link count and still be an orphan in substance.

Passes the information test because the audit reports what was found, not what was assumed. Do not
convert it into a checker validating a fixture.
