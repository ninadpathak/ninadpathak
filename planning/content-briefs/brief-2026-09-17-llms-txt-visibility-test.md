## Evidence — Experience B, and this is the hardest artifact in the campaign

A controlled test needs a pre-registered method, a matched comparison, and a defined observation
window. **This is a long-running experiment and it cannot be manufactured inside one run.**

If the observation window has not elapsed by the slot date, that is a legitimate reason to publish
the pre-registration instead: the method, the matched sets, what would count as a result, and the
date the answer is due. A stated method with no answer yet is honest and useful. **A conclusion
without the window elapsing is fabrication and must not ship.**

**Publish the null result if that is what happens.** The guardrail from the canonical plan stands:
never claim the file guarantees inclusion or ranking.
