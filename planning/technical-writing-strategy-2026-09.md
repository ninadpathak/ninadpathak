# Technical writing that earns the reader's next decision

Dated 10 September 2026. This is the local editorial decision record for the requested technical-writing niche strategy. Research is in [the Semrush evidence record](research/semrush-2026-09-10/README.md), with [ten briefs](technical-writing-briefs-2026-09.md) and [a URL decision register](technical-writing-url-decisions-2026-09.csv).

A readable instruction can still leave an engineer unable to act. The existing essay [Technical Writing Is Deciding What the Reader Can Assume](/articles/technical-writing-is-deciding-what-the-reader-can-assume/) demonstrates that problem with a timeout: the next request depends on whether the previous operation completed.

Build the site's position around **technical writing as the preservation of engineering decisions**. A page earns its place when it gives a reader a justified next action, or exposes a product promise that nobody can yet justify.

## Owning a specific job

The primary reader is an engineer or technical writer responsible for a developer product's documentation. The buyer is the documentation lead or founder who needs someone to investigate the product before writing about it.

The commercial promise is a process Ninad can demonstrate: trace a claim to product behavior, test the reader's path, and repair the explanation. Client outcomes require client evidence and permission before they become proof.

| Reader's situation | Useful deliverable | Business connection |
|---|---|---|
| A quickstart works only with help from its author | A reproducible onboarding test and corrected instructions | Documentation audit or rewrite |
| Reference pages omit recovery behavior | A tested failure path with explicit guarantees | API documentation engagement |
| Changes ship faster than docs can be reviewed | A change-to-page ownership record and a failing check | Documentation maintenance |
| An article sounds plausible but teaches the wrong model | An annotated claim review with a counterexample | Technical editing or developer education |
| A buyer needs to assess the writer | A public artifact with methods and corrections | Existing portfolio and contact pages |

AI systems are useful test subjects when they expose a writing problem. Generic model rankings and broad agent coverage do not receive new production slots merely because their keywords have volume.

## Turning the philosophy into editorial decisions

The site's September essays already carry the seed of this position. They distinguish what a test establishes from what a fluent sentence implies, and they label the export service as hypothetical.

| Principle | Editorial action | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| An instruction spends the reader's trust | Identify the assumption behind the next action | A reviewer can point to the source or test that supports it |
| A writer investigates the product | Ask what happens after timeout or restart before describing recovery | The responsible engineer confirms the guarantee or the prose names the uncertainty |
| Brevity has a downstream cost | Remove a passage, then retry the task | Removal does not force the reader to recover required information elsewhere |
| A failure needs provenance | Retain the failing input and the corrective diff | Another reader can reproduce the narrow failure |
| An essay can change its premise | Record the strongest objection before drafting recommendations | The final claim survives the objection or narrows visibly |
| A useful result can be small | Publish the tested boundary, including a null result | The title describes what was observed |

Paul Graham's [How to Write Usefully](https://paulgraham.com/useful.html) supplies a standard for precise claims that remain true. His [Writing, Briefly](https://paulgraham.com/writing44.html) supports rewriting as a way to develop an idea, with trusted readers finding confusing passages.

Simon Willison's [What to Blog About](https://simonwillison.net/2022/Nov/6/what-to-blog-about/) connects writing to things learned and built, including screenshots that survive a project's disappearance. Apply that habit through dated artifacts and runnable examples; borrowing either author's mannerisms would erase the judgments this site needs to establish.

The requested Paul Graham skill was not found by filename or content in the repository, `~/.agents/skills`, or `~/.codex/skills`. The existing `ai-memory-editorial-program.md` contains a Graham/Willison synthesis, so its writing principles informed this plan alongside the primary essays and the installed technical-writing skill.

## Applying an editorial gate

A lexical scan is necessary but cannot certify human authorship or good writing. The gate is a review of thought and evidence, with the technical-writing skill's banned vocabulary checked mechanically.

| Check | Pass | Failure branch |
|---|---|---|
| Central claim | A reviewer can state the specific decision the article changes | Rewrite the premise before polishing sentences |
| Sources | Consequential factual claims have primary sources beside them | Investigate or remove the claim |
| First person | Experience is confirmed by Ninad; new tests name who ran them | Reframe as observation or hypothesis, never an invented memory |
| Experiment | Commands, environment, raw output, exclusions, and limits are saved | Hold the result and publish no numerical conclusion |
| Mistake | A real failed attempt and correction exist | Omit the failure story; do not add a theatrical confession |
| Opening | The first paragraph begins with a recognizable problem or artifact | Delete promises and generic scene-setting |
| Consecutive sentences | Each pair advances the thought | Collapse deferred answers and repeated conclusions |
| Structure | Headings trace the reader's task; practical sections contain a check | Replace advice-only sections with a worked case |
| Voice | The writer makes a judgment and earns it | Remove stock oppositions, forced metaphors, and interchangeable headings |
| FAQ | Each question adds something a reader needs | Remove duplicated summary material |
| Code | A fresh environment reproduces the claimed behavior | Repair setup or narrow the example's label |
| Final review | An independent reader identifies no unsupported guarantee | Return to the same owner with exact findings |

The required claim ledger records `claim`, `kind`, `source_or_run`, `scope`, and `author_confirmation`. Kinds are sourced fact, observed test, author recollection, opinion, and hypothesis; moving between kinds requires new evidence.

## Measuring whether the work helps

Use existing GSC reporting conventions and record new page changes in a separate intervention log. Do not overwrite the daily measurement files while they carry active work.

| Signal | Collection and comparison | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Indexability | Build audit, canonical, sitemap, and live URL inspection after an authorized release | A regression blocks further releases until repaired |
| Search relevance | Same query-page pairs over completed 28-day windows; separate brand and classified fan-out | New unrelated queries trigger an intent review |
| Organic visits | GSC clicks, with withheld queries and classification limits disclosed | Do not substitute impressions or Semrush estimates for readers |
| Reader success | A consenting tester runs the task without author help; record completion and exact failures | Fix observed blockers before extending the series |
| Artifact use | Available download or tool events, with a baseline and event definition | Repeated useful actions justify maintenance time |
| Qualified demand | Manually attribute inquiries to a page where the reader supplies that information | Count relevant engagements separately from generic contacts |
| Earned discussion | Record unsolicited links and substantive objections | Use objections to revise the claim; do not score quality by upvotes |

At day 14, missing indexability evidence means investigate delivery before editing titles. At day 30, if reader tests still fail on the same prerequisite, stop new drafting and repair the instruction.

At day 60, if the two completed pieces exceeded the time budget by more than half, reduce cadence before adding another subject. If indexed refreshed pages have no relevant query observations, inspect intent and internal discovery; the sample is insufficient for a ranking-effect claim.

At day 90, retain pages with useful reader evidence even if search observations remain sparse. If the entire refreshed cohort has neither relevant search observations nor documented reader use, stop expansion and interview intended readers before another quarter of production.

A comparison with an unchanged cohort is descriptive because topics and starting positions differ. Avoid calling movement a causal effect, and record algorithm changes or concurrent site edits that could affect interpretation.

### Making a piece worth discussing

Prepare an artifact a skeptical engineer can inspect without a signup. State the result and its boundary in the title, make the method accessible, and leave room for someone to reproduce or challenge it.
