# Story and evidence backlog

These questions are deliberately not answered in public copy. Each could support a first-person story after Ninad confirms the facts.

## Product understanding

1. Which documentation page made you angry enough to fix the product understanding behind it, and what exactly was wrong?
2. Which tutorial changed after you ran the code and found the official or supplied path did not work?
3. When did you reject a brief because the requested article would have taught the wrong mental model?

## AI and writing

1. What is the clearest example of AI producing plausible technical prose that failed against the product?
2. Which parts of research, testing, and editing do you now hand to AI, and which decision do you keep for yourself every time?
3. Has a draft become less like you after an AI cleanup? Which sentence best shows what disappeared?

## Work and outcomes

1. Which published relationships may be described as client work, employment, partnership, or another specific role?
2. Which case-study metrics have source artifacts that can be cited or retained for private review?
3. Which testimonials and internal-sounding quotes in the existing work pages are verbatim, attributable, and approved for publication?

Until those answers exist, the site must not manufacture scenes, dialogue, roles, or outcomes to fill the space.
