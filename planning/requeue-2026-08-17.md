Indexing is not the constraint (86-91% earn rate, time-to-first-impression down to 4 days). CTR is
not the constraint (it is 5.9% of the volume). Cluster mix is closed, measured twice. **Position
is the constraint**, and position is mostly set by difficulty and by whether a page already exists.

## The survival test

A row survives as a new URL only if it passes one of these:

1. **Verified low difficulty with a resolved parent topic.** KD ≤ 25 with the parent topic owning
   itself, checked on a live SERP. That is the only profile with a credible route to the top 20 on
   a rebuilt domain.
2. **Structural**, where the page fixes something broken regardless of its own traffic.
3. **Credibility**, where the piece exists to prove first-hand ground and was never a traffic play.

Everything else is a lottery ticket and becomes a skip or an improvement.

## Final batch-1 direction after point-in-time measurement

**The first reversal is withdrawn. The original target, `ai-memory-management-for-llms`, remains
the owner.**

The first position report only listed pages that moved. Treating absence from that report as an
unknown position caused the reversal recorded in `e457d0ae`. The point-in-time instrument then
measured both pages on the same bases:

| Window | `ai-memory-management-for-llms` | `memory-hierarchy-in-ai-systems` |
|---|---:|---:|
| 28-day all-query position | 20.4 on 22 impressions | **18.2 on 5 impressions** |
| 90-day all-query position | **22.7 on 48 impressions** | 28.7 on 15 impressions |
| 90-day human-query position | **37.0 on 1 named query** | 87.0 on 1 named query |
| Inbound links | 8 | **13** |

The hierarchy page wins only the 2.2-position 28-day comparison on five impressions and inbound
links. Links are the input the merge itself retrofits, so using them to pick the destination is
near-circular. The owner page wins the longer all-query window, the like-for-like human-query
comparison, and impressions by more than three to one.

Therefore `memory-hierarchy-in-ai-systems`, `the-memory-hierarchy-why-rag-is-not-enough`, and
`memory-attribution-errors` merge into `ai-memory-management-for-llms`, as the original mechanical
spec already stated. Commit `ab04412c` placed the carried prose on the hierarchy source but added
no redirect and retired no page; that prose must be salvaged into the owner inside the batch, then
the source can be retired. No irreversible merge has happened.
