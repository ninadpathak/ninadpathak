Rules

What gets flagged

error
  • Paragraph length. A prose paragraph holds two sentences at the most.
  • Personal voice. A full article needs first-person experience or judgment. Anonymous reference copy belongs in product docs.
  • Em dashes. Replace each em dash (—) in prose with a comma, a period, or parentheses.
  • Contrastive parallelism. Flags "unlike X, Y…", "whereas", and "on the other hand". State the evidence instead of the contrast.
  • Horizontal lines. Replace a --- section divider with a heading or white space.
  • Banned jargon. Flags "leverage", "synergy", and "unlock". Name the action the word stands in for.
  • Banned sentence openers. Flags sentences that start with "In", "This", "By", "Finally", "Most", or "Ever".
  • Condescension words. Flags "simply", "just", "easy", "obviously", and "of course". Each one tells a stuck reader the step should not have stopped them.
  • AI writing signals. Flags "delve", "in conclusion", "it's worth noting", "rapidly evolving", and "harness the power". Readers take these phrases as a sign that a model wrote the page.
  • Filler openers. Flags a first line such as "In this article…" or "Today we will learn…". Open with the answer or the task instead.
warning
  • Hedge words. Flags "very", "really", "quite", and "basically". Each one weakens the claim it modifies.
  • Buzzwords. Flags "leverage", "seamless", "robust", "cutting-edge", and "utilize". Replace each with the property you can show.
  • Filler phrases. Flags "in order to", "note that", "please note", and "as you can see". Delete them and the sentence loses nothing.
  • Tutorial "we". Flags "we will" and "let's". Address the reader as "you".
info
  • Passive voice. Found by pattern and marked for review, because some passive sentences are the right choice.
  • Long sentences. Flags sentences over 35 words. Split them so a reader scanning the page gets one claim at a time.
  • High adverb density. Flags text where more than 5% of words end in -ly. Cut the adverbs that repeat what the verb already says.
  • Low readability. Flags a Flesch reading ease score below 30, the band the scale rates very difficult.
