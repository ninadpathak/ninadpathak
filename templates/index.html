Start with a writing problem

The instruction is clear. Can the reader act on it?
A timeout leaves a caller deciding whether to retry. The answer depends on a guarantee the documentation needs to explain.
A polished paragraph can hide an unfinished idea.
Use an AI-assisted review to question what the evidence supports before expanding the draft.
A documentation template needs a product behind it.
