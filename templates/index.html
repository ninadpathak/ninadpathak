What I do

Creating content engineers call “awesome”

I've worked with Semrush, Adobe, TinyFish, Mastra, Manicule, OpenComputer, and many other companies over the last eight years.

Working closely with their engineers, product marketers, product managers, founders, and CTOs means understanding both how the product works and where it's headed.

That becomes documentation that explains the engineering decisions and tutorials that show developers how to use them. DevRel becomes a PR function for your engineering team: the work behind the product gets understood outside the company.

01
Organic Discovery & Strategy

Find what developers search for when choosing tools. Plan content that answers those questions and shows where your product fits.

02
Documentation

Get developers from setup to a working first request. Explain what the API guarantees and how to recover when a request times out.

03
Developer Education

Teach developers to build with your product through working examples. Explain the tradeoffs so they can adapt the code to their applications.
