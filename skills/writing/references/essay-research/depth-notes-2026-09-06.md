# Depth and voice — 2026-09-06, pg-skill-codex2

## Reading scope and method

Whole reread of the first 40 essays in the supplied `/tmp/pg-codex/manifest.json`, plus *Beating the Averages*, *How to Write Usefully*, *Write Like You Talk*, and *Having Kids*: 44 distinct essays. The other requested titles are within the first 40. Read notes and acknowledgments too. Selection and source hashes are preserved in `/tmp/pg-codex/depth-pass/selection.json`. The manifest reports 106,267 words; whitespace counting can differ slightly. Long essays were read in consecutive 4,500-word chunks without skipping the middle. These are fresh reading judgments, not recycled assertions of coverage from the previous pass.

Each entry identifies a starting observation, a resulting idea, and the point where a new connection is earned. An early announced thesis is distinguished from the later step that supports or revises it. Quoted voice sentences are from the supplied local texts, with whitespace normalized. They are specimens for analysis, not instructions to copy their syntax. A compelling explanation can still have weak empirical premises; the notes flag consequential leaps rather than treating PG's confidence as verification.

## 01. How Universities Should Prepare Founders

Source: [essay](https://paulgraham.com/prepare.html), `prepare.txt`.

- **Starts from:** Universities send their graduates to YC, which selects people who can build things and already do.
- **Arrives at:** Preparing founders may require giving students time back, even when that makes the university look less active. A change that helps the energetic minority can improve the mean while leaving the median unchanged.
- **Earned step:** “The price of giving the most energetic students room” answers the university's strongest objection: free time will be wasted. He accepts that cost, then reasons from asymmetric room for improvement. Earlier, the reading-period examples make unstructured time a concrete condition: students together, nothing due tomorrow. Those examples suggest a mechanism; they do not prove that doubling reading period would double valuable companies. The application-rate argument also leaves selection and culture partly confounded.
- **Voice:** “We're like grad school.” / “They're used to telling themselves.” / “Turn a blind eye to it.” The short sentences depend on a specific institutional situation; they are judgments, not motivational decoration.

## 02. How to Earn a Billion Dollars

Source: [essay](https://paulgraham.com/earn.html), `earn.txt`.

- **Starts from:** A political claim conflicts with what he sees in a founder growing rapidly by pleasing customers.
- **Arrives at:** A few million and a billion need not require different kinds of conduct; sustained growth can connect them. He then connects seemingly random projects to future demand through the users who make them.

## 04. The Brand Age

- **Starts from:** The “quartz crisis” combined competition, exchange rates, and technological substitution. Some Swiss firms survived by selling prestige.
- **Arrives at:** Branding rewards distinction where useful design rewards convergence. Quality then changes from the selling advantage into a threshold. Artificial scarcity can put a manufacturer in the business of maintaining an asset bubble.

## 35. Write Simply

Source: [essay](https://paulgraham.com/simply.html), `simply.txt`.

## 43. Write Like You Talk

## What makes a smart reader feel he understands the thing?

He can say where an explanation stops working. The aspiring founder in *Billionaires Build* can answer detailed questions about users and name what might go wrong; the pitch merely claims promise. PG's essays repeatedly offer the equivalent of those answers. He notices a detail an attractive account leaves out, tries to explain it, and lets the explanation change the original question. The Forbes comparison becomes a question about 1982. The Airbnb founders change his interview standard. His happy parenthood has to survive the objection that becoming a parent changed the judge. These are consequential tests of his idea, including tests he could lose.

A familiar starting point is useful because it gives the reader something against which to check the reasoning. A story supplies the constraint, decision, and result rather than mere warmth. A number supplies a discrepancy rather than authority. An example can defeat the generalization that introduced it. An implication is worth following when it changes what someone would look for or do: an interview should inspect knowledge; a competitor's job listing can reveal its technical choices; a useful nonprofit may need allocation freedom. The reader acquires a way to understand another case, not only a verdict about this one.

This does not make every PG assertion well supported. The wealth essays' broad moral conclusions, some cultural prevalence claims, and numerical flourishes can outrun their evidence. Confidence, memorable cadence, and firsthand standing are not substitutes for sampling or causal identification. Borrow the habit of looking closely enough to revise a claim. Do not borrow certainty as a voice effect.

## What is the relationship between plainness and depth?

Plainness makes the reasoning inspectable by both writer and reader. Writing down what he means forces a choice that a vague thought can postpone; saying it in ordinary language exposes the missing reason or the condition that makes the claim false. He can then repair the thought. In *Good Writing*, even a line break starts that search; in *How to Write Usefully*, qualification improves the idea; in *Write Like You Talk*, elaborate language is dangerous partly because it makes the writer feel they have said more than they have. The finished explanation feels inevitable because the revisions have removed unsupported turns and left the observations that make the next step follow. It was not inevitable to discover.

Plainness also preserves a person doing the thinking. “We hoped that would save us” distinguishes a bet from retrospective destiny. “I thought in Basic” includes the author in the limitation he explains. “It hurts to see that sentence written down” makes a concession cost him something. A smart reader can hear someone judging, regretting, testing, and changing their mind without being taught the basics of their own work. Ordinary vocabulary is the surface result. The deeper discipline is making a claim definite enough to be useful and exposed enough to be corrected.

## Consequences for the rewrite

Restore stories, close observations, changed questions, model repair, counterexamples, and further implications as choices made for a reason. Tie each to a local quotation and its surrounding argument. Preserve the 106-essay hook/reasoning archive as evidence of variety, including its uncertainty and corrected readings; do not restore its mandatory sequence or rhetorical forms. Restore the role of discovery during drafting: a candidate idea is useful, but an unanswered, well-grounded question can be a legitimate starting point. Deliver only after the draft has made progress on it.

A short introduction should let the reader recognize the work and see a worthwhile inquiry. It need not demonstrate all the depth the body will eventually earn. One-shot quality means doing the private thinking and revision before delivery, not pretending a single unexamined generation can replace them. The trials must therefore judge both the quality of the thought and whether anyone would say the prose aloud.
