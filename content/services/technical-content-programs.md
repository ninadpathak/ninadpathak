## What a program covers

- **The topic plan.** What to publish and in what order, drawn from what your buyers ask and what people search for.
- **The review process.** Who checks each draft against the product before it's published.
- **The writers.** Hiring, vetting and technical review when the work needs more than one person.

## How I plan the topics

I start with your customers. Your calls and sales call transcripts tell me what buyers keep asking, and I use Claude and other AI tools to pull those topics together.

Then I check search demand in Semrush and put the two lists together. After pages are live, I track them in Google Search Console and adjust the plan.

## How I run the writing

Every piece gets checked against the product before it ships. At Mem0 I hired writers who understood the subject, so drafts were accurate before they reached review.

## FAQ

**What is a technical content program?**

It's the topic plan, the review process and the writers behind a company's technical articles, run as one system so every piece is checked against the product.

**Do you write the articles or manage writers?**

Both. I write individual pieces, and I hire and manage writers when the plan needs more than one person.

**How do you measure a content program?**

I track the pages in Google Search Console after they go live and adjust the plan from what they rank for.
