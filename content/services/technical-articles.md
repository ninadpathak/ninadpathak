## What I write

- **Concept explainers.** They define a term an engineer has half-met and show how it works.
- **Comparisons.** They help an engineer choose between tools, with the tests or sources behind each claim.
- **Best-of roundups.** They compare the options in a category on the features an engineer cares about.

## How I choose topics

I start from what your buyers ask. I listen to your calls, ask for sales call transcripts, and use Claude and other AI tools to summarise the topics people keep raising.

Then I check the search demand in Semrush. A topic goes on the list when your buyers ask about it and people search for it.

## How I write it

I run the products an article is about, yours and the ones it's compared with. A claim I can't check gets a source and a date, or it comes out.

## How we'd work together

Email [hello@ninadpathak.com](mailto:hello@ninadpathak.com) with the product and the reader, or book a 30-minute call at [https://zcal.co/ninadpathak/meet](https://zcal.co/ninadpathak/meet). This page lists no rate, so ask for a quote with the scope.

1. We talk for 30 minutes about the reader and what the article has to do for them.
2. You get a written brief and a timeline within 24 hours of that call.
3. I need a demo account, API keys and an account with real data in it, because I run the product before I write about it.
4. Once the brief is agreed, the first piece is a paid trial, and we carry on only if you're happy with it.
5. A draft takes about 5 days from the brief and revisions take 2, so you can publish in one working week.
6. There are two rounds of review at most.
7. I deliver in Google Docs or as a Markdown pull request, or in whatever your team uses, and I can load it into your CMS if you'd like.

Once we've worked together for a while, the same cycle comes down to 4 days end to end.

After an article is live, I track it in Google Search Console and report what it ranks for.

## FAQ

**What do technical article writing services include?**

Topic research, a brief, the article and up to two rounds of review, with the products in the article run before it's written.

**How do you pick what to write about?**

From your sales calls and customer conversations first, then from search demand checked in Semrush.

**Do you use AI to write?**

I use AI to prepare research and to attack a draft. I check the product myself before I sign off on a claim.
