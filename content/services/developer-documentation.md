## What I write

- **Quickstarts.** One path from a clean machine to a first working result, with the requirements stated before the first command.
- **Reference pages.** The request, the response and the errors for each endpoint or command, checked against the running product.
- **Troubleshooting pages.** What a developer sees when something fails, what caused it and what to do next.

## How I write it

I run every command on the page before it goes to you. A step that fails for me would fail for your reader, so it gets fixed or explained before the draft exists.

Each step says what the reader should see when it works. That line is what lets a developer, or a coding agent, tell a working step from a broken one.

## What you get

You get pages your engineers can review for accuracy without rewriting them, in your docs format.
