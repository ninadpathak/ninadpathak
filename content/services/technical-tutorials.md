## What I write

- **Setup and integration tutorials.** A developer connects your product to a tool they already use and sees it work.
- **Build tutorials.** A developer builds something small and real with your product, start to finish.
- **Framework guides.** One task shown properly in one framework, with that framework's conventions.

## How I write it

I build the project before I write a word. The tutorial is then a record of something that worked, in the order a developer would do it.

Every step shows its expected output. If the reader's screen doesn't match, they know which step to go back to.

## What you get

You get a tutorial with code that ran, in your format, so your engineers review working code.

7. I deliver in Google Docs or as a Markdown pull request, or in whatever your team uses, and I can load it into your CMS if you'd like.

Once we've worked together for a while, the same cycle comes down to 4 days end to end.
